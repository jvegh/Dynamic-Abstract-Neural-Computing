1.1 Disciplinary layers

The material has multiple layers. In addition to the sections intended to be understandable by everyone (i.e. primarily readers with a suitable physiological and neuroscience background), there are also panels (typically in another chapters) for those with the relevant background, containing the necessary mathematical 1.1 and physical 1.1 knowledge, which require at least the appropriate level of BSc knowledge to understand, but sometimes knowledge that goes beyond this, and sometimes knowledge and explanations that make you delve deeper into the unusualness of the ideas. This method naturally leads to some redundancy, but enables the audience to read only the text his/her background qualifies for. We strive to make the material understandable without those supplementary material, and with them, the reader with the appropriate qualifications will be able to gain knowledge that encourages further thoughts.
