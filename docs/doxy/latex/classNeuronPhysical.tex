\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Implements a general physical-\/type neuron. 

The neuron collects membrane\textquotesingle{}s, synaptic and artificial currents, for details see \doxylink{classNeuronInputCurrent}{Neuron\+Input\+Current}.

In the corresponding states, the neuron is in one of its \doxylink{classGenCompStageMachine__t}{Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t} states
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Computing computing (collects charge)
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Delivering\+: delivers action potential
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Relaxing\+: restores resting potential
\end{DoxyItemize}

The Heartbeat\+\_\+method() handles an internal \textquotesingle{}state update\textquotesingle{} signal. In the corresponding stages, the neuron is
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Relaxing\+: Until it reaches its resting potential, calls \doxylink{classNeuronPhysical_a3e6514fed4b7c3bee8d54e64ecfbb736}{Calculate\+\_\+\+Do()}. Upon receiving an input, changes to stage Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Computing. Inputs enabled\+: the relative refractory period.
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Computing\+: Until it reaches its threshold potential, calls Calculate\+\_\+method(). At that point, mebrane passes to stage Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Delivering. Inputs enabled.
\item Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Delivering\+: Until it reaches the threshold membrane potential again calls Calculate\+\_\+method(). (The neuron delivers its result to its output section.) After terminating, it passes to stage Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Relaxing, without restoring membrane\textquotesingle{}s potential. At the beginning \textquotesingle{}Transmitting\+Begin\textquotesingle{} is also issued. (Sends \textquotesingle{}Begin Transmitting\+Begin\textquotesingle{}) \begin{DoxySeeAlso}{See also}
\doxylink{classscGenComp__PU__Abstract}{sc\+Gen\+Comp\+\_\+\+PU\+\_\+\+Abstract}.
\end{DoxySeeAlso}
Signal transmission happens in parallel with the further processing. Inputs disabled; the absolute refractory period.
\end{DoxyItemize}

--These stages below are momentary states\+: need some action and passes to one of the above stages
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Neuron\+Physical\+::\+Input\+Received\+\_\+method()\+: the unit received new input. Only administrative action; the received input is handled in Heartbeat\+\_\+\+XXX\+\_\+\+Do(), as described in \doxylink{classNeuronPhysical_a3e6514fed4b7c3bee8d54e64ecfbb736}{Calculate\+\_\+\+Do()}. Disabled in mode Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Delivering.
\item Neuron\+Physical\+::\+Synchronize\+\_\+method()\+: deliver immediate spike, anyhow ; (a momentary state) Passes to Gen\+Comp\+Stage\+Machine\+\_\+t\+::gcsm\+\_\+\+Relaxing (after issuing \textquotesingle{}Delivering\+End\textquotesingle{}) 
\end{DoxyItemize}
